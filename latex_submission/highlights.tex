\noindent \textbf{\large Highlights}

\vspace{8pt}

\begin{itemize}\itemsep6pt
\item Independent rodent-to-human directional gene-effect agreement is about 62\%
\item Shared-publication co-curation inflates naive cross-species agreement to about 89\%
\item Human-rodent divergence barely exceeds the mouse-rat agreement benchmark
\item Molecular endpoint and replication depth, not chemical class, govern transfer
\item Protein sequence conservation is not associated with directional transfer
\end{itemize}
